1. Executive Summary & Audit Overview

A deep technical and architectural audit was performed on the BCA Vault platform. A total of 64 findings across 4 key dimensions (Bugs, Performance, Security, and Code Quality) were identified. High-risk security loopholes (unprotected email administrative routes and quiz interfaces), race conditions in reward redemptions, unhandled outside-click listeners, broken navigation targets, and data table name mismatches have been resolved and validated through a clean production build.
